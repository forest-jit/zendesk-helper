What it does, and how to use it

Read messages, reply, save fields, and check your queue.

Start here

Set Zendesk to Light mode first: open your Zendesk profile picture → Appearance → Light mode. Choose Light mode rather than Match system appearance. Native Zendesk Dark mode is not supported yet and can leave mismatched colors. You can still choose a dark theme in the extension; your Mac’s appearance can stay as it is.

  1. Open the extension from Chrome’s toolbar.
  2. Use the On / Off switch. Off restores Zendesk’s native interface; your settings are kept.
  3. Choose a theme or open Workflow settings. Changes save immediately.

Themes and previews

Want to… Do this
Try a built-in theme Open Theme. Hover or move through options with arrow keys to preview the popup. Click or press Enter to apply. Escape cancels.
Follow your Mac’s appearance Choose System light / dark.
Make your own theme Open Create or edit a theme. Name it and pick Page background, Fields & messages, Text, Buttons & links, and Internal notes. Colors and hex values both work.
Try it on the ticket first Click Preview on ticket. Zendesk and enabled Harvest styling update temporarily. Stop preview or close the popup to return to your saved theme.
Keep or change it Choose Save and apply. To edit, select the saved custom theme and reopen the editor. Delete removes that custom theme. Unsaved editor colors are kept for the current Chrome session.

Each theme starts with five colors. Borders, hover states, and other shades are derived from them. Unreadable color combinations cannot be saved; adjust text against both backgrounds if the editor reports a contrast problem.

Match Harvest to your theme

Harvest styling is off by default. Choosing a theme does not turn it on.

  1. Open Jones IT Zendesk Helper from your browser’s toolbar.
  2. Click Match Harvest to theme.
  3. Allow access to platform.harvestapp.com when asked.
  4. Check that the button says Harvest theme on. If the permission prompt closed the popup, reopen it and click Match Harvest to theme again if needed.
  5. Reload the Harvest panel using its reload icon, or refresh Zendesk after protecting your drafts.

Set this up separately in each browser/profile and when switching between Store and unpacked installations. If Harvest stays white, check the setting and allowed site access. The helper changes styling only, not timer values or time entries. Other embedded apps keep their own interface.

Read a ticket

  • One reading flow: conversation history and the reply box share the left scrollbar. Scroll to the bottom to write. The right sidebar scrolls independently. The desktop columns have no divider line.
  • Short history: the latest five messages appear by default. Show older messages reveals the rest; Show latest 5 messages collapses them again.
  • Compact fields: Requester, Assignee, Followers, Tags, Ticket Code, Priority and Status stay in the main sidebar. Additional fields opens the rest.
  • Images: message images fit inside compact previews without cropping. Click a preview to open the full image; Escape closes it.
  • Titles: the automatic-title setting uses the selected company and requester: Company - Requester - Issue. It keeps the issue text and avoids repeated prefixes. Changes save with the ticket, not in the background. Undo restores the previous title and turns automatic updates off.

If company or requester information is missing, the helper leaves the title alone. It does not guess names. Keep the issue after the first two “ - ” separators when editing a structured title.

Email threads and readability

Want to… Do this
Shorten an email inside a message Choose Hide quoted email history. Show quoted email history reveals it again. This is separate from showing older ticket messages.
Reduce a bulky email layout Choose Simplify email formatting on a formatted email. Restore email formatting returns its original layout. It removes fixed widths, table padding and background fills in that message. Plain paragraphs may look almost unchanged; formatted content farther down may become shorter. It does not collapse quoted threads.
Check an inline answer Expand the quoted history. Replies written inside an earlier email can be part of that history.
Understand the initial view Clearly marked quotes can start collapsed when a new response exists outside them. Plain forwarded chains start open because their boundaries are less certain. Quote-only and unrecognized messages stay visible.

The helper changes the display only. Original text, links and images remain in place. Raw exports include rendered quoted history even when collapsed. Turning the helper off removes the disclosures and restores the native view.

With a theme selected, unreadable text against solid message backgrounds receives a contrasting display color. Readable authored colors and branded email panels stay intact. Complex image backgrounds remain original.

Other reading controls

  • Closed tickets: Status shows Closed and is disabled when Zendesk reports that the ticket cannot be edited. Helper Send and Save actions are hidden.
  • Native SLA badge: a negative time such as -23h indicates an overdue SLA target. It is separate from ticket status; Zendesk’s metric details identify the target.
  • Desktop columns: the helper uses a fixed sidebar width. Turn off Conversation left, ticket fields right or the helper to return to native column resizing.
  • Long Harvest references: notes and reference text use bounded scroll areas so the timer stays open and fields have more room. The helper does not change their values.

Reply and save

Action How
Switch reply mode in one click Use Internal note or Public reply beside the native selector. The native selector remains available for other channels.
Carry your text across modes Check Carry this draft. Text and formatting carry when the other draft is empty or still matches the last carried copy. A different destination draft is kept, with a message explaining the conflict. Separate drafts remain the default.
Send a reply or note Click Send, or press ⌘ + Enter while typing in the composer. Check the displayed public/internal mode first.
Save fields without sending Click Save changes, or press ⌘ + Enter while focused in the sidebar. The helper protects and restores the active text draft during the field save. Save stays disabled when nothing changed.
Finish a ticket Choose Solved. Save becomes Close ticket; Close and export also downloads the ticket. Both record Zendesk’s Solved status, not its later Closed status.

On other platforms, use Ctrl + Enter.

Quiet feedback

Successful copying, draft carry, settings changes and exports do not add a confirmation banner. The changed control, draft or Chrome download shows the result. Errors, draft conflicts, recovery instructions, saving progress and title Undo remain visible when needed.

If something interrupts a save

The helper keeps a local recovery copy. It will not overwrite a newer draft. If you see Save recovery copy, use it before continuing; this saves the held versions locally and keeps the current draft. Check the result before retrying an unconfirmed save.

Queued attachments must finish or be removed before carrying a draft or saving only fields. Carrying a draft never sends it.

Queue and activity

  • Copy ticket: hover over it near the title to open the choices; move away to close them. Choose number, link, title/link, or Markdown link. Keyboard: Enter or Space opens the choices, Tab reaches each action, Escape closes. Touch users can tap.
  • Viewing or editing: the small circle near the title shows an eye for reported viewing/open-tab activity and a pencil for editing. Hover or focus it for the names and status Zendesk reports.
  • Unknown activity: a question mark and “Other agents · unknown” mean the helper has not found a native viewing or editing signal. Hover or focus the question mark for an explanation. Someone may still have the ticket open. Older versions call this “not reported.” Native collision reporting depends on the Zendesk plan and the other agent’s session.
  • Queue refresh: Workflow settings offers 10, 15, 30 or 60 seconds, or Off. The default is 15 seconds. It uses the native view refresh; ticket pages never reload.
  • Refresh pauses: while you type, select tickets, open a menu/filter/dialog, interact recently, go offline, or hide the tab. The queue shows whether it is running or paused.

Activity uses Zendesk’s native collision reporting. Our eye/pencil icons summarize information read from Zendesk’s rendered indicators. Zendesk normally shows agent pictures in the upper-left ticket area: regular for viewing, blue outline for editing, dimmed when away. Views can show an eye. No separate presence service is added. Recognition of this account’s actual indicators still needs a two-person test.

Help test it

Keep real customer tickets read-only. Use #209867 for draft edits, field changes, saves or sends.

  1. With a coworker, open #209867 in separate accounts. Compare Zendesk’s native pictures with the helper badge while one person views, types without sending, switches away and closes the ticket. Check names and stale-state cleanup.
  2. Try different public and internal drafts with Carry this draft on. A distinct destination must survive; conflicts should explain what happened.
  3. On the test ticket, save a sidebar field while a reply draft exists. Confirm that the field saves without sending or losing either draft.
  4. On messy real tickets, try quote controls, Simplify/Restore and image previews. Check text, links, image access, colors and scroll position.
  5. Try your usual screen size, zoom, keyboard and assistive tools. Report the ticket number, theme, action and what you expected.

Presence results remain unverified until the coworker test passes. “Not reported” means unknown.

Export for local AI

  1. Use Export ticket for local AI in the popup, or Close and export after choosing Solved.
  2. Run the local processor on the raw JSON. It uses the installed Ollama text and vision models.
  3. Review notion-draft.md and processed.json for remaining personal information, missing media and incorrect interpretations.
  4. Import only the reviewed draft into the documentation service you choose.
python3 scripts/process_ticket.py "/path/to/ticket.raw.json"

Raw exports contain ticket information. They include rendered messages, including collapsed ones; unloaded history and inaccessible images are reported as outside the capture. Local AI scrubbing is best-effort. Image interpretations remain unverified until you review them. The helper does not publish to Notion or send ticket contents to a hosted AI service.

Installation and updates

Load the unzipped extension folder through chrome://extensions → Developer mode → Load unpacked. After an update, protect your drafts, reload the extension, and refresh Zendesk once. Team installation may need your organization’s approval.

For detailed local-processing and team-install instructions, see docs/DOCUMENTATION_HANDOFF.md and docs/DISTRIBUTION.md in the project folder.
